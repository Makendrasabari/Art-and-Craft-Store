history Our Journey

From Red Clay Kilns to a Global Digital Marketplace

Chatly was born from an unyielding reverence for indigenous techniques. Traversing across the pottery hills of Suleja, the royal weaving benches of Bonwire, and the bronze foundry quarters of Benin City, we witnessed masters creating museum-worthy heirlooms — yet constrained by hyper-local barter markets.

We engineered Chatly to dismantle the middlemen monopolies. By pairing modern digital storefronts and global climate-controlled logistics with traditional artisan cooperatives, we directly link craftspeople to art patrons around the globe.

Bogolan & Mudcloth Dyeing
