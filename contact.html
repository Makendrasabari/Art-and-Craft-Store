Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify the authentic origin and handcrafting of an item? expand_more
Every creation sold on Chatly comes with an authentic Serialized Certificate of Provenance signed by the master artisan. We personally vet and inspect our artisan guild studios to guarantee traditional methods without synthetic industrial shortcuts.
Can I request a custom or bespoke commission from an artist? expand_more
Yes! You can contact the artist directly through your Buyer Dashboard or select "Custom Commission" in the contact form above. We facilitate direct communication, dimensional sketches, and safe escrow payments until you approve photographs of the finished piece.
What is your fragile art shipping and breakage insurance policy? expand_more
All ceramic vessels, wood carvings, and delicate crafts are encased in multi-layer biodegradable cellular shock cushioning and sealed timber crates. In the rare event of transit damage, our 100% white-glove transit insurance provides immediate complimentary re-creation or full refund.
How are artisans compensated? expand_more
Unlike traditional dealers who take 50-70% markups, Chatly remits over 85% of the purchase price directly to the artisan. Payouts are made instantly via local mobile wallets (M-Pesa, Wave) or direct bank transfer upon shipment confirmation.
How do digital art downloads and commercial licenses work? expand_more
Upon purchase, digital prints and 3D assets are instantly unlocked in your Buyer Dashboard under "Downloads". Standard purchases include non-commercial fine-art display rights; extended commercial printing licenses can be selected during checkout.
How do I apply to become an artisan seller on Chatly? expand_more
Simply navigate to our Sign Up Page, select the "Seller" role, and submit 3 photos of your handcrafted pieces. Our curatorial council reviews applications within 48 hours.
